# Ord Words 字

A picture word app for a 5 year old learning **Svenska**, **English** and **中文**. It's a single web page with no build step and no dependencies.

## What's inside

- **Words:** 10 picture decks (fruit, animals, food, colours, body, people, school, outside, numbers and picture characters like 日 月 山 水). Tap a word to hear it, or tap the picture to hear all three.
- **Listen & find:** hear a word, tap the right picture. "Mix" mode is half Chinese, with Swedish and English mixed in so nothing gets forgotten. After two wrong taps the word is said in Swedish as a hint, then in Chinese again.
- **Say it:** hear the word, record yourself saying it, then hear yourself and the model.
- **Phonics (English):** letter sounds, blending (c · a · t → cat), reading words under a picture, and tricky words (the, is, said...).
- **Stickers:** every 5 stars unlocks a new sticker.
- **Grown-ups** (press and hold): progress per language, settings, and **record your own voice** for any word or letter sound. Recordings stay on the device and are used instead of the device voice.

Words she finds harder come up more often in the games.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Host it with GitHub Pages

Go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then pick `main` and `/ (root)`.

## Tips

- The device voice for Chinese and Swedish depends on the phone or tablet. On iPad, install better voices under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
- Single letter sounds from the device voice are rough. Recording your own in Grown-ups makes phonics much better.
